# Threadlight

Carry a spark through twenty stars. Your spark circles its current star and projects a dotted flight path. Send it when that path crosses the next star's ring. The small gold center awards extra points, and consecutive perfect connections build a bonus.

Press **Space**, click, or tap anywhere to send the spark. All three do the same thing. Use that same control to start and restart. You have three sparks; a missed shot or waiting fifteen seconds costs one. Later stars have smaller rings and drift, while your spark orbits faster.

The game uses procedural canvas artwork and synthesized sound. It runs directly from `index.html`, without dependencies or downloads. Your best score is saved locally when browser storage is available. Sound starts with the first interaction.
