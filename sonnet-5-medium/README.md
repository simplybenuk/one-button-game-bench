# Cinder Drift

A small ember drifts between stars. Each star traps you in an orbit that slowly collapses toward its burning core, so you can't stay long.

## Control

One action: `Space`, click, or tap anywhere. It always does the same thing: **release** the ember from its orbit. It flies in a straight line along the dotted guide, the tangent at the moment you let go.

## Goal

- Time each release so the ember's straight path hits the ring of the next star, which captures it.
- Each new star scores a point. Capture within one lap of the previous star to build a streak, and each streak step adds a bonus point.
- You die if your orbit decays into the core, you fly through a core, you touch a black hole, or you drift too far without landing.
- After a death, the same button restarts the game.

Best score is saved locally.
