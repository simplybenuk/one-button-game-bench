# Kindle — Opus 5.5 Medium

You are a small ember climbing a dark sky by jumping between lanterns.

## The one control

**Space**, **tap** or **click** all do the same thing: **leap**.

- While you circle a lantern, pressing the button launches you in a straight line along your current direction of travel.
- Pass close to another lantern and it catches you. You then circle it at the distance you passed by, clockwise or anticlockwise depending on which side you came in on.
- The same button starts the game and restarts it after you die.

## Goal

Light as many lanterns as you can and climb as high as possible.

- **Fuses:** a lantern only burns for a few seconds once you reach it. If it burns out while you're on it, you get flung off in whatever direction you happen to be facing.
- **Aim for the flame:** passing within a hair of a lantern's core scores a **PERFECT**. Chained perfects raise your multiplier, and a loose catch resets it. Tight catches also make you circle faster, so the next leap is harder to time.
- **Embers** float between lanterns and give bonus points. **Gold lanterns** sit off the main route: they're worth 5× but have shorter fuses.
- **Shades** (dark violet wisps) drift across your route. Touching one snuffs you out.
- You die if you miss every lantern, drift out of the column or fall below the screen.

Speed, lantern spacing, moving lanterns and shades all increase as you climb. Your best score is saved locally.

A single self-contained `index.html` with canvas rendering and WebAudio sound, and no external assets.
