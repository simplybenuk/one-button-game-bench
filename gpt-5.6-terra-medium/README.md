# PULSE//ORBIT

**PULSE//ORBIT** is a one-button orbital survival game. You pilot a tiny signal skimmer around a dying star, switching between two orbital lanes to collect blue echoes while avoiding orange debris.

## Controls

Press **Space**, click the game, or tap anywhere to phase between the inner and outer orbit. The skimmer travels on its own; time each phase change to line up with echoes and miss debris. After a crash, use the same control to restart.

## Goal

Keep the signal chain alive, collect echoes for score, and survive increasingly dense orbital fields. A perfect chain charges a temporary slow-motion "focus" state.
