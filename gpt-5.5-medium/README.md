# Nullwake

Nullwake is a one-button survival game about diving through a hostile two-lane wake. Red fractures block one lane while the other lane opens for a clean crossing. The score climbs with distance, and precise crossings build a chain multiplier.

## Control

Use one action for everything:

- Press `Space` on desktop
- Click with a mouse
- Tap anywhere on mobile

The action shifts the diver to the opposite lane. After a crash, the same action restarts the run.

## Goal

Read the incoming gates, shift at the right moment, and stay in the open lane as the field speeds up. Yellow gates are higher-value crossings, so keeping a chain through them is the fastest way to push a deeper score.
